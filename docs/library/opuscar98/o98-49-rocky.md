## 风格提示词（中文原文）

70 年代 35mm 写实，放在细致的 3D 世界里，默认日出（日落同理）。太阳离地平线五到十度，暖橙 #F0A060，从侧面或前侧方照亮主角，让受光的身体和拉过地面的长影看得清；不要完全逆光。天空冷蓝 #7FA3CF，云底染粉 #E6B2AE；雾朝太阳一侧发金、背光一侧发蓝紫。调色：细颗粒（±3%），暗部偏冷、高光偏暖，不过曝；只有太阳入画时才有光晕和鬼影。镜头：低机位斯坦尼康从背后跟着主角爬上一段坡或台阶，到最高处接一个连续环绕，边转边升边退，六到八秒，转到受光的脸，直到整个地方在下面铺开。对标 John G. Avildsen《洛奇》（1976）、斯坦尼康操作者 Garrett Brown。避免：正午平光、没细节的剪影、高潮时机位不动、塑料感反光。

## Style prompt (English original)

1970s 35mm realism in a detailed 3D world, sunrise by default (sunset the same). Sun 5–10° up, warm orange #F0A060, lighting the hero from the side or three-quarter front so the lit body and long ground shadows read; no full backlight. Cool blue sky #7FA3CF, pink cloud undersides #E6B2AE; haze golden toward the sun, blue-violet away. Grade: fine grain (±3%), cool shadows, warm highlights, nothing clipped; flare and ghosts only with the sun in frame. Camera: a low Steadicam following the hero from behind up an ascent, then at the top one continuous orbit that circles, rises and pulls back over 6–8 s, showing the lit face, until the place spreads out below. References: John G. Avildsen, *Rocky* (1976); Steadicam operator Garrett Brown. Avoid: flat noon light, detail-less silhouettes, a static climax, plastic CG shine.

## 这个风格是什么（上游说明）

清晨的费城整个做成 3D，从肉库到博物馆 72 级台阶，调成 70 年代 35mm：冷蓝天、低低的金色太阳、长影子、薄雾、细颗粒。跑上台阶和顶上的环绕升镜是这部片的记忆点。

A full 3D Philadelphia at sunrise, from meat locker to museum steps, graded like 1970s 35mm: cold blue sky, low gold sun, long shadows, haze, fine grain. The run up the 72 steps and the rising orbit at the top are what people remember.

## 上游示范用法（只是一个例子，不要照搬故事和镜头）

Clawd 扮洛奇：灰色卫衣兜帽戴起，肩上搭一条白毛巾。训练蒙太奇三个快切，约 1.7 倍速；上台阶以后回到 1 倍。硬切进斯坦尼康跟跑，之后不再切：跑到顶直接接环绕升镜，举臂一跳正好落在镜头开始升起的时候。

| 机位 | 镜头 | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| meat | 肉库中景，定机位：戴灰色兜帽的 Clawd 对着挂着的牛肉出拳，白瓷砖、裸灯泡。 | Meat locker, medium shot, fixed camera: Clawd in a grey hood jabs at a hanging side of beef among white tiles and bare bulbs. |
| meat_close | 更近一些，定机位：拳头打在牛肉上，牛肉晃荡。 | Closer, fixed camera: the punches land and the beef swings. |
| street | 黎明前的意大利市场，定机位：Clawd 迎着镜头慢跑，经过油桶火、果蔬摊和还亮着的路灯。 | The Italian Market before dawn, fixed camera: Clawd jogs toward the lens past fire barrels, fruit stands and lit streetlamps. |
| run | 低机位斯坦尼康从背后追着 Clawd 跑上 72 级台阶，前方是博物馆的立柱。 | A low Steadicam chases Clawd from behind as it runs up the 72 steps toward the museum's columns. |
| orbit | 到顶，Clawd 双臂举成 V 字一跳；镜头绕着它转、上升、后退，直到整条大道和城市在日出里铺开。 | At the top Clawd throws its arms up in a V and hops; the camera circles round it, rising and pulling back until the whole Parkway and the city open up at sunrise. |

参考：John G. Avildsen, Rocky (1976)；Garrett Brown (Steadicam)；James Crabe (cinematography)；Philadelphia Museum of Art, east façade
